# Drag & drop scene art

Open a scene folder → open **`DROP/`** → drag your A1111 PNGs in as:
`0.png` `1.png` `2.png` `3.png`

Copy that layer's `N_POSITIVE.txt` into A1111 first. Same NEGATIVE + SETTINGS for all layers.
